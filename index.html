Connection

Petty Cash Members

The people who receive petty cash. Add as many as you need. Used in the Petty Cash mode and the "Paid Via" tag on Purchases bills.

Device storage

Once a bill is safely saved to your Sheet, its local copy on this device is just extra weight — this clears that out without touching anything in your Sheet or Drive. Use this if you ever see a "storage full" error.

Fix date formatting

Protects the Date column on every existing tab so Google Sheets can never silently swap day/month (e.g. 11/06 becoming 06/11). One tap covers all tabs — no need to do this by hand in Sheets.

Add a new month tab

Creates a fresh tab in your Sheet with the right headers, ready for a new month. Its Date column is protected automatically.
